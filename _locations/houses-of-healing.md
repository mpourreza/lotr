*The Houses of Healing* · Third Age

## Overview & Essence

The Houses of Healing are the hospital wards of Minas Tirith, where the wounded of the Pelennor are brought. Aragorn enters as a healer, not yet crowned, and saves those struck by the Black Breath. The herb kingsfoil (athelas) is his sign.

## Key Characteristics & Attributes

- **Type/Category:** Hospital / sanctuary
- **Origins/Creation:** Ancient institution of Gondor in the sixth circle of the City.
- **Key Affiliations/Associations:** Aragorn; Faramir; Éowyn; Merry; Ioreth the talkative wisewoman.
- **Primary Role/Purpose:** To mend the critically wounded when medicine alone fails.

## Detailed History & Narrative Arc

- **The Black Breath.** Wounds from the Witch-king and his weapons.
- **The hands of the king.** Aragorn's healing proves his right as much as his sword.

## Notable Connections & Relationships

- **[Minas Tirith]({{ '/locations/minas-tirith/' | relative_url }}):** City that contains them.
- **[Aragorn]({{ '/characters/aragorn/' | relative_url }}):** Healer-king.
- **[The Battle of the Pelennor Fields]({{ '/events/pelennor-fields/' | relative_url }}):** Source of the wounded.

## Legacy & Significance

Healing here is political theology: the king's touch is part of his office in Gondor's lore.
