*The Buckland; the East-march* · Third Age

## Overview & Essence

Buckland lies between the Brandywine and the Old Forest, settled by the Oldbucks (later Brandybucks). Brandy Hall at Bucklebury is its centre. Merry is of this country; the High Hay fences the Forest. Frodo's first night out of the Shire proper is at Crickhollow.

## Key Characteristics & Attributes

- **Type/Category:** Region (Hobbit march)
- **Origins/Creation:** Settled T.A. 740 by Gorhendad Oldbuck; later the Brandybuck chieftains.
- **Key Affiliations/Associations:** Merry; Brandy Hall; the Ferry; Old Forest border.
- **Primary Role/Purpose:** To guard the Shire's eastern flank and to house a bolder Hobbit strain.

## Detailed History & Narrative Arc

- **Crickhollow.** Frodo's supposed new home; the conspiracy unmasked.
- **The Ferry.** Escape from a Black Rider on the Road.

## Notable Connections & Relationships

- **[Meriadoc Brandybuck]({{ '/characters/meriadoc-brandybuck/' | relative_url }}):** Master of Buckland after the War.
- **[Old Forest]({{ '/locations/old-forest/' | relative_url }}):** Wood beyond the Hay.
- **[The Shire]({{ '/locations/the-shire/' | relative_url }}):** Realm it marches with.

## Legacy & Significance

Buckland Hobbits are slightly less insular than the Shire average—a fit birthplace for Merry's later courage.
