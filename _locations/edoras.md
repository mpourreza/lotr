*Seat of the Golden Hall* · Third Age

## Overview & Essence

Edoras is the capital of Rohan, a town of thatch and wind on a hill, dominated by Meduseld, the Golden Hall. It is exposed on the plain and close in memory to Helm’s Deep. Gandalf’s confrontation with Wormtongue occurs here.

## Key Characteristics & Attributes

- **Type/Category:** City / royal seat
- **Origins/Creation:** Built as the Mark’s seat after the gift of Calenardhon; Meduseld is the king’s hall.
- **Key Affiliations/Associations:** Théoden; the royal house; Gandalf as guest and healer of the king.
- **Primary Role/Purpose:** Political and symbolic centre of Rohan.

## Detailed History & Narrative Arc

- **Decline.** Under Gríma the hall is a place of shadow.
- **Waking.** Théoden rises, Gríma is expelled, and the war-host rides.

## Notable Connections & Relationships

- **[Rohan]({{ '/locations/rohan/' | relative_url }}):** The kingdom it heads.
- **[Théoden]({{ '/characters/theoden/' | relative_url }}):** Its king.
- **[Gandalf]({{ '/characters/gandalf/' | relative_url }}):** Who restores the king in Meduseld.

## Legacy & Significance

Edoras is the starting gate of Rohan’s war, not a siege-city on the scale of Minas Tirith.
