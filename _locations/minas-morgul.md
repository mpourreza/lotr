*Tower of the Moon; the Dead City* · Second Age to Third Age

## Overview & Essence

Minas Morgul is the city Isildur built as Minas Ithil, later taken when the Ringwraiths returned. It glows with a sickly light. Frodo and Sam see it from the cross-roads; the Witch-king leads his host out to war against Minas Tirith.

## Key Characteristics & Attributes

- **Type/Category:** Fortress-city (corrupted)
- **Origins/Creation:** Built by Isildur; fallen to the Nazgûl in the Third Age.
- **Key Affiliations/Associations:** The Witch-king; Frodo and Sam; Gollum; the pass into Mordor.
- **Primary Role/Purpose:** To guard the eastern pass and to be the launching place of the Morgul-host.

## Detailed History & Narrative Arc

- **The cross-roads.** Statues and turning toward the Morgul Vale.
- **The march.** The Witch-king's army pours toward Osgiliath and the Pelennor.

## Notable Connections & Relationships

- **[Isildur]({{ '/characters/isildur/' | relative_url }}):** Who built it as Minas Ithil.
- **[The Witch-king of Angmar]({{ '/characters/witch-king/' | relative_url }}):** Lord of the city.
- **[Cirith Ungol]({{ '/locations/cirith-ungol/' | relative_url }}):** Pass nearby into Mordor.
- **[Minas Tirith]({{ '/locations/minas-tirith/' | relative_url }}):** Twin city still held by Gondor.

## Legacy & Significance

Minas Morgul is the anti-Minas Tirith—same origin, opposite master. The book uses it to show Gondor's long defeat in the East.
