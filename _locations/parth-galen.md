*The Green Sward* · Third Age

## Overview & Essence

Parth Galen is the grassy sward below Amon Hen on the west bank of Nen Hithoel. The Fellowship lands here after leaving Lórien. Debate, temptation, death, and capture happen in one afternoon—the narrative hinge of the whole War.

## Key Characteristics & Attributes

- **Type/Category:** River lawn
- **Origins/Creation:** Natural clearing on the Anduin bend above Rauros.
- **Key Affiliations/Associations:** The Fellowship; Boromir; Uruk-hai of Isengard.
- **Primary Role/Purpose:** To be the campsite where the company ends as a single story.

## Detailed History & Narrative Arc

- **Camp.** Boats hidden; paths chosen or argued.
- **Breaking.** Boromir dies; Merry and Pippin taken; Frodo and Sam gone east.

## Notable Connections & Relationships

- **[Amon Hen]({{ '/locations/amon-hen/' | relative_url }}):** Hill above the lawn.
- **[The Breaking of the Fellowship]({{ '/events/breaking-of-the-fellowship/' | relative_url }}):** What happens here.
- **[Boromir]({{ '/characters/boromir/' | relative_url }}):** Who falls defending the younger Hobbits.

## Legacy & Significance

Parth Galen is the last green place before the Quest splits into threads the book will follow separately.
