*Citadel of the Stars* · Second Age to Third Age

## Overview & Essence

Osgiliath was the chief city of Gondor, spanning the Anduin. The palantír of the chief stone was lost here. Sauron's forces take the eastern half; Faramir fights a fighting retreat. The Morgul-host crosses on its way to the Pelennor.

## Key Characteristics & Attributes

- **Type/Category:** Ruined city / crossing
- **Origins/Creation:** Founded by Isildur and Anárion; capital until the kin-strife and plagues.
- **Key Affiliations/Associations:** Faramir; Boromir; the Anduin; Minas Tirith upstream.
- **Primary Role/Purpose:** To be the contested ford between East and West in the War's Gondor chapter.

## Detailed History & Narrative Arc

- **Faramir's defence.** Rangers hold the western ruins until overwhelmed.
- **The crossing.** The Witch-king's army passes toward the city of the Kings.

## Notable Connections & Relationships

- **[Faramir]({{ '/characters/faramir/' | relative_url }}):** Captain who retreats from its banks.
- **[Minas Tirith]({{ '/locations/minas-tirith/' | relative_url }}):** City upstream that depends on its defence.
- **[The Anduin]({{ '/locations/anduin/' | relative_url }}):** River it bridges.

## Legacy & Significance

Osgiliath's fall in the book is the drumbeat before the siege—not a spectacle city but a wound in Gondor's body.
