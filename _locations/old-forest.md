*The Old Forest* · Third Age

## Overview & Essence

The Old Forest is a remnant of Eriador's ancient woods, hostile to trespassers. Old Man Willow sleeps at its heart; the Withywindle runs through it. The Hobbits take a shortcut and are trapped until Tom Bombadil sings them free.

## Key Characteristics & Attributes

- **Type/Category:** Ancient forest
- **Origins/Creation:** Predates the Shire; once part of a vast woodland; the High Hay keeps it from Buckland.
- **Key Affiliations/Associations:** Tom Bombadil; Goldberry; Old Man Willow; the four Hobbits (early).
- **Primary Role/Purpose:** To be a living barrier and a reminder that Eriador was once all trees.

## Detailed History & Narrative Arc

- **The path.** The Hobbits lose the path and circle toward the River.
- **The Willow.** Merry and Pippin are trapped until Tom arrives.

## Notable Connections & Relationships

- **[Tom Bombadil]({{ '/characters/tom-bombadil/' | relative_url }}):** Master who walks here unharmed.
- **[Buckland]({{ '/locations/buckland/' | relative_url }}):** Neighbour separated by the Hay.
- **[Barrow-downs]({{ '/locations/barrow-downs/' | relative_url }}):** Hills beyond its eastern edge.

## Legacy & Significance

The Forest is not evil in Sauron's sense—it is old and resentful. That distinction matters in Tolkien's ecology.
