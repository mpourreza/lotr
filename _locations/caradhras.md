*Redhorn; Caradhras* · Third Age

## Overview & Essence

Caradhras is the mountain the Fellowship tries to cross from Hollin. Snow and wind mount; Boromir's suggestion to use the Ring is shouted down; Gandalf fears a will in the storm. They turn back to the Dimrill Gate and Moria—a choice that costs Gandalf.

## Key Characteristics & Attributes

- **Type/Category:** Mountain pass
- **Origins/Creation:** One of the great peaks of the Misty Mountains above Khazad-dûm.
- **Key Affiliations/Associations:** The Fellowship; Legolas and Gimli's argument about the mountain's name; the storm.
- **Primary Role/Purpose:** To bar the high road and force the lower road into darkness.

## Detailed History & Narrative Arc

- **The climb.** Pack-ponies lost; snow buries the path.
- **The turn.** Moria chosen instead; the Balrog waits.

## Notable Connections & Relationships

- **[Moria]({{ '/locations/moria/' | relative_url }}):** Alternative road taken after failure here.
- **[The Fellowship of the Ring]({{ '/events/fellowship-of-the-ring/' | relative_url }}):** Company turned back.
- **[Gandalf]({{ '/characters/gandalf/' | relative_url }}):** Who reads malice in the storm.

## Legacy & Significance

Caradhras is the book's first major fork: over the mountain or through the mines. The mountain answers for them.
