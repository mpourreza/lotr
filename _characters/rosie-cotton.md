*Rose Cotton; Sam's Rosie* · Third Age

## Overview & Essence

Rosie Cotton is a Hobbit of the Shire, daughter of Farmer Cotton, whom Samwise Gamgee loves long before the Quest. Her presence in Sam's mind—especially near Cirith Ungol—helps him resist despair. After the War they marry, raise a family at Bagshot Row, and Sam becomes Mayor.

## Key Characteristics & Attributes

- **Type/Category:** Character (Hobbit)
- **Origins/Creation:** Born in Bywater; known to Sam from youth; sister to Tom, Jolly, Nick, and Nibs Cotton.
- **Key Affiliations/Associations:** Samwise Gamgee; the Cotton family; the Shire after the Scouring.
- **Primary Role/Purpose:** To represent home and ordinary happiness Sam fights to restore.

## Detailed History & Narrative Arc

- **Before the Road.** Sam thinks of her garden and her smile when the Quest is darkest.
- **Return.** She helps heal the Shire; she and Sam wed when the year turns.
- **After.** Many children; Sam's mayoral years; she survives into his later life.

## Notable Connections & Relationships

- **[Samwise Gamgee]({{ '/characters/samwise-gamgee/' | relative_url }}):** Husband and Ring-bearer's companion.
- **[Bywater]({{ '/locations/bywater/' | relative_url }}):** Village of her family.
- **[The Shire]({{ '/locations/the-shire/' | relative_url }}):** Land they rebuild together.

## Legacy & Significance

Rosie is not a warrior but she is a motive. The book's ending ties victory to marriage, gardens, and children—not only to thrones.
