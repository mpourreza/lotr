*March-warden of Lórien* · Third Age

## Overview & Essence

Haldir is an Elf of Lothlórien who meets the Fellowship at the borders and enforces the law of the Golden Wood: strangers enter blindfolded. He guides them toward Caras Galadhon. In the book he does not march to Helm's Deep; that is a film addition.

## Key Characteristics & Attributes

- **Type/Category:** Character (Elf; Galadhrim)
- **Origins/Creation:** Serves the lords of Lórien as border-warden in the Third Age.
- **Key Affiliations/Associations:** Galadriel; Celeborn; the Fellowship as guided guests.
- **Primary Role/Purpose:** To guard the Wood's secrecy and to conduct lawful guests to the lords.

## Detailed History & Narrative Arc

- **The Naith.** He requires even Legolas and Aragorn to be blindfolded for parity with Gimli.
- **The march.** In the book his role ends in Lórien; film sends him to die at Helm's Deep.

## Notable Connections & Relationships

- **[Lothlórien]({{ '/locations/lothlorien/' | relative_url }}):** Wood he guards.
- **[Galadriel]({{ '/characters/galadriel/' | relative_url }}):** Lady of the realm he serves.
- **[The Fellowship of the Ring]({{ '/events/fellowship-of-the-ring/' | relative_url }}):** Company he escorts inward.

## Legacy & Significance

Haldir is a small but memorable voice for Lórien's closed policy. Readers should not import film-only deaths into book canon.
