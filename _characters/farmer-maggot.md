*Farmer of the Marish* · Third Age

## Overview & Essence

Farmer Maggot is a Hobbit of the Shire's Marish, a farmer who owns dogs feared by local Hobbits and knows Tom Bombadil. He has no love of Black Riders and speedily ferries Frodo, Sam, and Pippin toward Buckland when the Nazgûl hunt begins.

## Key Characteristics & Attributes

- **Type/Category:** Character (Hobbit)
- **Origins/Creation:** Lives at Maggot's Farm in the Eastfarthing; grows mushrooms; knows the Old Forest's border.
- **Key Affiliations/Associations:** Frodo; Sam; Pippin; Tom Bombadil; the Black Riders on the Road.
- **Primary Role/Purpose:** To aid the travellers without joining the Quest.

## Detailed History & Narrative Arc

- **The farm.** Frodo steals mushrooms in youth; Maggot later gives them freely.
- **The Riders.** He describes a Black Rider at his gate and helps the Hobbits escape by boat.

## Notable Connections & Relationships

- **[Frodo Baggins]({{ '/characters/frodo-baggins/' | relative_url }}):** Traveller he ferries toward Buckland.
- **[Buckland]({{ '/locations/buckland/' | relative_url }}):** Destination of the night ride.
- **[Tom Bombadil]({{ '/characters/tom-bombadil/' | relative_url }}):** Friend he knows from the border country.

## Legacy & Significance

Maggot is early proof that the Shire's ordinary folk can be brave without knowing the whole tale.
