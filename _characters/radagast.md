*Radagast; the Brown* · Third Age

## Overview & Essence

Radagast is one of the Five Wizards, called the Brown for his love of wild things. He lives at Rhosgobel between Mirkwood and the Anduin. Gandalf sends him as messenger to Orthanc in a trap; Saruman uses the errand. He sends eagles and news that aid the hunt for the Ring.

## Key Characteristics & Attributes

- **Type/Category:** Character (Maia / Istar)
- **Origins/Creation:** Came with the Istari about T.A. 1000; settled in Rhovanion.
- **Key Affiliations/Associations:** Beasts and birds; Gandalf; Saruman (unwilling go-between); the eagles.
- **Primary Role/Purpose:** To tend the living world; his messages and beasts indirectly serve the wider war.

## Detailed History & Narrative Arc

- **Rhosgobel.** A house among trees where he knows every nest and burrow.
- **The errand.** Gandalf's note to Saruman passes through him and leads to Gandalf's imprisonment.
- **The eagles.** Beasts carry word; Gwaihir later bears Gandalf from Orthanc.

## Notable Connections & Relationships

- **[Gandalf]({{ '/characters/gandalf/' | relative_url }}):** Fellow Istar who trusts his beasts.
- **[Saruman]({{ '/characters/saruman/' | relative_url }}):** Who exploits his simplicity.
- **[Gwaihir]({{ '/characters/gwaihir/' | relative_url }}):** Eagle who aids Gandalf.
- **[The Istari]({{ '/people/istari/' | relative_url }}):** Order to which he belongs.

## Legacy & Significance

Radagast is not a comic footnote in the book: he is the reason Gandalf reaches Isengard. His failure is distraction from the war of counsels, not cowardice.
