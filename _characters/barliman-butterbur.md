*Master of the Prancing Pony* · Third Age

## Overview & Essence

Barliman Butterbur is the Man who keeps the Prancing Pony in Bree. He forgets to send Gandalf's warning letter to Frodo—a failure that nearly costs everything. After the Nazgûl attack he helps with ponies and news and later receives the returned Bill the Pony.

## Key Characteristics & Attributes

- **Type/Category:** Character (Man of Bree)
- **Origins/Creation:** Long keeper of the chief inn on the East Road; honest but absent-minded.
- **Key Affiliations/Associations:** Gandalf's letter; Frodo's company; Nob the hob and Bob the ostler; Bree-land.
- **Primary Role/Purpose:** To host travellers and, belatedly, to speed the Ring-bearer on the Road.

## Detailed History & Narrative Arc

- **The letter.** Gandalf's note warning Frodo to leave at once—never delivered in time.
- **The attack.** Strider hides the Hobbits; Butterbur helps with beds and ponies afterward.
- **Bill.** The pony bought here returns after the Quest.

## Notable Connections & Relationships

- **[Bree]({{ '/locations/bree/' | relative_url }}):** Town of his inn.
- **[Frodo Baggins]({{ '/characters/frodo-baggins/' | relative_url }}):** Traveller he failed to warn in time.
- **[Aragorn]({{ '/characters/aragorn/' | relative_url }}):** Strider who takes charge at the Pony.

## Legacy & Significance

Butterbur's forgetfulness is comic and consequential. Tolkien lets him be good-hearted without being competent—a mirror of the Shire's unpreparedness.
