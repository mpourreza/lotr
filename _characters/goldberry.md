*Daughter of the River* · Unknown age

## Overview & Essence

Goldberry is the wife of Tom Bombadil, called River-woman's daughter. She welcomes Frodo's company to their house with water-lilies in her hair. Her presence is calm, seasonal, and without fear of the Ring. She represents the Withywindle's gentler face beside Tom's wild mastery.

## Key Characteristics & Attributes

- **Type/Category:** Character (unknown kind)
- **Origins/Creation:** Associated with the River Withywindle and Tom's house at the edge of the Old Forest.
- **Key Affiliations/Associations:** Tom Bombadil; the Hobbits as guests; the Withywindle.
- **Primary Role/Purpose:** To keep Tom's house and to offer hospitality without entering the War.

## Detailed History & Narrative Arc

- **The welcome.** 'The water lilies have come to you'—a meal and a night's safety.
- **The morning.** She sends them on toward the downs where danger waits.

## Notable Connections & Relationships

- **[Tom Bombadil]({{ '/characters/tom-bombadil/' | relative_url }}):** Companion and lord of the household.
- **[Old Forest]({{ '/locations/old-forest/' | relative_url }}):** Country near their home.
- **[Frodo Baggins]({{ '/characters/frodo-baggins/' | relative_url }}):** Guest before the Barrow trial.

## Legacy & Significance

Goldberry appears only in this episode. She is not a combatant or counsellor in the War; her role is restorative interlude.
